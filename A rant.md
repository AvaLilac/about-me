# hey

So, a few days ago, on the 28th of July, I had chemo.
My brain gets blasted with radiation to fight off cancer. Stoat, the platform Avia Client is for, decided the day I had chemotherapy was the day they would release their new for-web. Now, that's fine. The fucking issue is they minified every single CSS class they had. So what happened? Well, Avia Client depended on those CSS classes to remain reliable.
So the for-web update completely broke the client entirely. After chemo, I feel incredibly sick. I usually remain bedridden for the day as I recover. But because of this, I had to force myself to get off my sick ass, get on my computer, and start coding. Now, thankfully, I am not the only one on this dev team. If it was not for Amelia, I would not have been able to fix this client. I was barely able to do anything myself. I couldn't even really see the screen. I was emotional, under a lot of stress because of the for-web.
But Amelia found ways to start fixing stuff. So I started to fix stuff as well, even with my current state at the time, which will bite me in the ass later. I spent the entire day coding instead of resting, something that is not good for my body in any way.

### Aftermath

And it being broken has caused me so much stress. I'm still feeling most of that stress today. When I know for a fact they didn't have to minify it, then have it regenerate the classes on every single for-web release. That is not needed. They did that when they didn't even need to.
I'm sorry about this rant. I needed to get that off my chest because I'm still fucking pissed off about it. The one day I needed rest, and I couldn't get any because of this. And I know I could have waited to fix things. But a lot of people know I prefer to get working stuff as fast as possible. It's in my personality. So I am incredibly pissed off at them for this, so I wanted to rant.
